What PASS means

This is software integration and finite-field stability screening. Coarse 5120-cell WALE/DES/high-Re runs do not validate physical accuracy. A 10 s duration alone does not prove statistical convergence or a credible Strouhal number. The smaller time step in Round 3 is a regression case, not a complete time-step independence study. Saved output spacing 0.5 s differs from internal adaptive integration time steps.
